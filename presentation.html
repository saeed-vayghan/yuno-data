11 · Detection

Remove the FX move first, then judge the gap

expected = auth × rate(settle day) / rate(auth day)   ·   residual = settled − expected

Money done right

  • Integer minor units; CLP has 0 decimals
  • Flag = meaningful + large (~14%)
  • Outlier = a large row, no z-score
  • Cut-offs live in thresholds.yaml

19 · Honest limits

What this does not prove yet

⚠️ Limits

  • Synthetic data: the truth check scores our rules against the generator, not the real world
  • Action $ counts only excess vs peers (~$12k/qtr), not the whole $129k; most loss is structural
  • PSP adjustments (33% of loss) need PSP statements to explain, not just rates
  • The CFO's 18% flag rate is not reproduced (we flag 14%)
  • Optional stacks need 4–6 GB Docker; run one at a time on 2 GB

➡️ Next

  • Real PSP settlement files through recon ingest (per-PSP formats)
  • Order events to confirm partial captures (R1)
  • Dashboard shows alert history and "provisional" weeks
  • Real fee contracts in the dated psp_fees seed
  • Then the AWS path: S3/Iceberg, StarRocks, MWAA, Managed Flink

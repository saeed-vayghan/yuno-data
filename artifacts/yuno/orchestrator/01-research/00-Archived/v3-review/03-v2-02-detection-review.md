## Debate highlights
- ⚡ **Kaveh:** P4 is a brief pattern, and your own signature example is ".00". But those rows are `rounding`, below the flag, and you only cluster flagged rows. The headline pattern is invisible.
- 🏛️ **Jamshid:** Fair hit. The flag measures the size of the problem. Causes must look at every row where Δ ≠ 0. I'll change both rows 3 and 4.
- ⚡ **Kaveh:** The $0.50 rounding floor turns a 4.9% gap on a $10 basket into noise. The brief already gives us the floor: "small" starts at 0.1%.
- 🏛️ **Jamshid:** Agreed. Using the brief's number is easier to defend in the interview than our own dollar cut-off.
- ⚡ **Kaveh:** The MAD z-score: two-thirds of the rows are exact, so the median and the MAD are both 0. z is 0 divided by 0.
- 🏛️ **Jamshid:** Score only non-exact rows, with the mean-deviation fallback. It is one WHERE clause, and 07's outlier alert keeps working.
- ⚡ **Kaveh:** The brief wants a simple tool. Why ship a tree with a holdout and HDBSCAN on top of the GLM and signatures?
- 🏛️ **Jamshid:** Drop the tree. HDBSCAN stays optional, one purity table, because Q5 literally says "clusters". We don't build it if time is short.
